# 1.5.1

### New
- When you first sign in to FDV, the app offers to bring in your FDV flights. You'll see what it found before anything changes.
- New flights from FDV now come in on their own when you open the app (at most every 30 minutes). **Sync new flights** in Settings still checks right away.
- After an update, this screen shows what changed.

### Fixed
- Filling in air times no longer starts over partway through. PIREPs that have no air time on FDV are checked once and then left alone, so you can enter those yourself.
