# Backup and export

Keep your logbook safe and take it elsewhere.

## Backing up your logbook
<!-- keywords: backup, back up, save logbook, new phone, uninstall -->
Your logbook lives only on this device. Back it up regularly, and always before uninstalling the app or switching devices.

1. In **Flight History**, tap **⋮** then **Back Up Logbook**.
2. Choose where to save the file, such as Google Drive or your Downloads folder.

The backup contains every flight and your profile photo. It doesn't contain your FDV sign-in.

## Restoring from a backup
<!-- keywords: restore, import backup, recover -->
In **Flight History**, tap **⋮** then **Restore from Backup** and pick a backup file.

Restoring merges the backup into your logbook. Flights you already have are skipped, so restoring the same backup twice doesn't create copies. A photo in the backup is only used if you don't already have one.

## Exporting to CSV
<!-- keywords: csv, spreadsheet, excel, google sheets, export -->
In **Flight History**, tap **⋮** then **Export CSV**. The file has one row per flight with every field, for use in a spreadsheet. While a search or filter is narrowing **Flight History**, the menu reads **Export CSV (number shown)** and exports just those flights, for example one month's logbook. Clear the search and filters to export everything.

## Exporting to PDF
<!-- keywords: pdf, print, logbook pdf, export -->
In **Flight History**, tap **⋮** then **Export PDF** for a printable logbook with one line per flight. It follows a search or filter the same way as CSV.

## Sharing an export
<!-- keywords: share, send, email -->
When an export or backup finishes, tap **Share** on the message at the bottom of the screen to send the file with any app.
