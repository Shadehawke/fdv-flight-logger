# Flight history

Find, review and reuse the flights in your logbook.

## Searching and sorting
<!-- keywords: search, find, filter, sort, newest, oldest -->
In **Flight History**, type in the search box to find flights by airport, airline, flight number (including DAL numbers), aircraft, route, procedures, notes, FDV status or landing rating, such as "butter".

The sort button switches between newest first and oldest first.

## Filtering your history
<!-- keywords: filter, filters, date range, last 30 days, airline, aircraft, flight type, pending, rejected, not on fdv, clear -->
The chips under the search box narrow the list:

- **Date:** the last 7, 30 or 90 days, this year, or a custom range from the calendar
- **Airline** and **Aircraft:** every value in your logbook, most-flown first, with a count
- **Type:** online or offline
- **FDV status:** approved, pending, rejected, or never filed on FDV

Pick as many values in one filter as you like: Airline DAL and AFR shows both. Different filters narrow each other: DAL, A321 and the last 30 days shows only flights that are all three. The search box applies on top.

Tap **Clear** to remove every filter. The line above the list shows how many flights match out of your whole logbook.

## Flight details
<!-- keywords: detail, view flight, open flight -->
Tap a flight to see everything recorded for it, section by section. If it's linked to FDV, the PIREP number and its review status are shown at the top. Flights with a landing rate show its rating, such as Smooth, here and in the history list.

## Return leg and duplicate
<!-- keywords: return leg, reverse, duplicate, copy, same flight again -->
From a flight's detail page:

- **Fly return leg** (arrows icon) starts a new flight with the airports swapped
- **Duplicate** starts a new flight with the same details

## Editing and deleting
<!-- keywords: edit, change, delete, remove flight -->
From a flight's detail page, tap **Edit** to change it, or **Delete** to remove it. Deleting can't be undone, so the app asks first.

## Route map
<!-- keywords: map, route map, routes, world, network, airports, great circle, zoom -->
The route map draws your flights as lines across the world, along the curved shortest path an aircraft really flies. Busier routes are thicker and brighter, airports are sized by how often you've been there, and your hub is ringed.

- **Pinch** to zoom, **drag** to move, **double-tap** to zoom in. The map wraps sideways like a globe.
- **Fit** (top right) shows all your routes again.
- **Tap an airport** to see how often you've been there and your top routes from it.

The filters work here too. Open the map from **Flight History** (the globe icon) and it starts with the same filters, so you can map one month's flying or one aircraft. It's also linked from **Your logbook** on the dashboard.

The map is built into the app and works offline. A flight is left off the map if one of its airports isn't in the app's airport list; the line above the map says how many.
