# The dashboard

Your pilot card, logbook statistics and what's happening on FDV, at a glance.

## Your pilot card
<!-- keywords: rank, next rank, hours, flights, streak, profile, card -->
When you're signed in, the card at the top shows your FDV profile:

- **Rank** and a bar showing your progress to the next rank, with the hours still to go
- **FDV Hours:** your total as FDV counts it, with a breakdown of hours flown, bonus hours and transfer hours
- **Flights:** your approved FDV flights
- **Flight Streak:** your current streak and your best

Tap the card to open your account in **Settings**, where the refresh button updates it from FDV.

## Flight streaks
<!-- keywords: streak, best streak, broken -->
Your flight streak is counted in flights, as FDV counts it. Any flight that isn't an event, group flight or jump seat flight breaks the streak.

## Logbook gauges and statistics
<!-- keywords: gauges, stats, statistics, block hours, online, hub, milestones, top aircraft, top routes, longest -->
Under **Your logbook**, the gauges show:

- **Flights** and **block hours**, filling toward the next milestone
- **Online share:** how many of your flights were flown online
- **Hub share:** how many flights started or ended at your hub

Below them are your total distance, airports visited, longest flight, top aircraft and top routes, and your last flight. Tap the last flight to open it.

These figures come from your logbook in this app, so they include flights you logged without filing them on FDV.

## What counts toward your stats
<!-- keywords: pending, rejected, approved, not counted, stats different, hours don't match -->
Like FDV, the dashboard counts **approved** flights only. Flights that are pending review or were rejected on FDV are left out, and a note under the gauges says how many.

Flights you logged but never filed on FDV, or haven't synced yet, are your own logbook and do count.

Your last flight always shows your most recent flight, even while it's pending.

> Your FDV Hours on the pilot card can still differ from the Block Hours gauge. FDV adds bonus and transfer hours, and only counts flights you filed with FDV.

## Event card
<!-- keywords: events, live, next event -->
When an FDV event is live, the dashboard shows it with a LIVE badge and when it ends. When nothing is live, it shows the next event and when it starts. A tick means you're registered.

Tap the card to see all events.

## Update card
<!-- keywords: update, new version, upgrade -->
When a new version of the app is available, a card on the dashboard says so. Tap **Update** to download and install it, or **What's new** to read the release notes. See **Settings and updates** for more.
