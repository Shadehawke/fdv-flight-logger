# Settings and updates

Your profile photo, preferences and keeping the app up to date.

## Your profile photo
<!-- keywords: photo, picture, avatar, image, profile picture -->
In **Settings**, tap the photo on your account card and choose **Choose photo**, **Change photo** or **Remove photo**. The photo appears on your pilot card and is included in your backups.

## Settings at a glance
<!-- keywords: settings, preferences, keep screen on, simbrief alias -->
In **Settings** you'll find:

- **FlyDeltaVirtual account:** sign in or out, refresh your profile, and logbook sync
- **SimBrief / Navigraph Alias:** needed for SimBrief import
- **Keep Screen On:** stops the screen sleeping while you log a flight
- **Theme and units**
- **App updates**

## App updates
<!-- keywords: update, install update, new version, download, check for updates -->
The app checks for a new version once a day. When there is one, the dashboard shows an update card.

1. Tap **Update**. The app downloads the update and checks it's genuine.
2. Android asks you to confirm the install. The first time, it may ask you to allow this app to install apps.
3. The app restarts on the new version. Your logbook is kept.

To check yourself, go to **Settings**, **App updates**, **Check for updates**.

> If an update fails to download or install, see **Troubleshooting**.
