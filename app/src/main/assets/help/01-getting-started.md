# Getting started

Set up the app, sign in to FlyDelta Virtual, and find your way around.

## Welcome
<!-- keywords: about, overview, what is -->
FDV Flight Logger is a logbook for your flight-sim flights. Log each flight as you fly it, from the departure runway to the landing flaps.

Signed in with your FlyDelta Virtual (FDV) account, the app also brings in your bookings, PIREPs, rank, awards and events. Without an account, it works as a standalone logbook.

> Your logbook is stored only on this device. Back it up regularly: see **Backup and export**.

## First-time setup
<!-- keywords: setup, pilot id, callsign, hub, name, first run -->
The first time you open the app, it asks for your pilot profile and preferences.

1. Tap **Sign in with FDV** to fill in your Pilot ID, name and hub automatically, or type them in yourself.
2. Choose your theme and units for weights, fuel, QNH and temperature.
3. Tap **Finish Setup**.

You can change your preferences at any time in **Settings**.

## Signing in with FDV
<!-- keywords: login, log in, account, vatsim, password, token, sign in -->
Signing in lets the app read your FDV profile, bookings, PIREPs, awards, events and fleet.

1. Tap **Sign in with FDV** in Setup, or in **Settings** under FlyDeltaVirtual account.
2. FDV's sign-in page opens in your browser. Sign in with your email and password, or with VATSIM.
3. You're taken back to the app, signed in.

The app never sees your FDV password. It receives a sign-in token that lasts 30 days, stored encrypted on this device. When it expires, sign in again.

The app only reads from FDV. It never files, changes or deletes anything on your FDV account.

## Getting around
<!-- keywords: navigation, icons, toolbar, top bar, menu -->
The dashboard is the app's home screen. The icons in its title bar take you to:

- **Events** (calendar): FDV events happening now and coming up
- **Trophy Case** (trophy): your FDV awards and trophies
- **Settings** (gear): your account, preferences, updates and this help
- **Flight History** (list): every flight in your logbook

Events and the Trophy Case appear once you're signed in to FDV.

Under your pilot card, **Start new flight** opens a new logbook entry and **Open saved flight** takes you to your flight history.

## Themes and units
<!-- keywords: dark mode, light mode, theme, lbs, kg, inhg, hpa, celsius, fahrenheit, units -->
In **Settings** you can choose:

- **Theme:** FDV, Light or Dark
- **Weight Units (Payload/ZFW)** and **Fuel Units (Fuel/Reserve):** pounds or kilograms
- **QNH Units:** inches of mercury or hectopascals
- **Cruise OAT Units:** Celsius or Fahrenheit

SimBrief imports and FDV actuals are converted to your units automatically.
