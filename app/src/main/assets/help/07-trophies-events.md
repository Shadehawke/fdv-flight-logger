# Trophy Case and events

Your FDV awards, trophies and events.

## The Trophy Case
<!-- keywords: trophies, awards, trophy case, achievements, locked, earned, rarity -->
Tap the **trophy icon** on the dashboard to open your Trophy Case. It has two tabs:

- **Awards:** FDV's award artwork in frames, grouped by category. Earned awards show the date; locked ones are greyed out with what it takes to earn them.
- **Trophies:** on shelves by rarity. A number such as ×18 shows how many times you've earned one.

Tap any award or trophy for its description, requirement and date. Turn off **Show locked** to see only what you've earned.

## Events
<!-- keywords: events, group flight, fly in, register, upcoming, live -->
Tap the **calendar icon** on the dashboard to see FDV's events, under **Happening now** and **Upcoming**.

Each event shows its route, times in your local time and Zulu, how many pilots are registered, and whether you are. Group Flight, Fly In, Fly Out and In or Out events are marked **CAT Free · double hours**.

You register for events on FDV's website: tap **Register on FDV** or **View on FDV** to open the event's page.
